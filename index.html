About Jackie

Hi, I'm Jackie. A steady, down-to-earth therapist for a very fast world.

Licensed Professional Counselor · Over a decade in the field

Jackie McGreggor is a Licensed Professional Counselor in Texas and the founder of Modern Balance Therapy, with more than ten years across private practice, counseling centers and community programs.

Her approach is evidence-based and shaped to each person, with practical tools you can use between sessions.

Read Jackie's full bio

Jackie built the practice around a simple observation: the modern world asks a lot of people, and most of us were never taught how to put any of it down.

Over more than a decade she has worked in private practice, counseling centers, correctional facilities and community programs, with people from every walk of life. She works with teens from age 12, adults, seniors and couples, and her favorite work is with people in their 20s and 30s.

Her style is eclectic and evidence-based, shaped to each person rather than the other way around. Expect a safe, nonjudgmental space, real conversation, and someone who will walk alongside you rather than talk at you.

Questions

The things people usually want to know first.

Does online therapy actually work?

For the kinds of concerns this practice focuses on, yes. Video sessions let you talk from a place you already feel comfortable, and people tend to skip fewer sessions when there is no commute. All you need is a private spot, decent wifi and headphones.

Where do I need to be during sessions?

Physically in Texas. Licensing rules follow the client's location, not the therapist's. Anywhere in the state works.

Is it confidential?

Yes. Sessions run on a secure, HIPAA-compliant video platform and your records stay within the practice. The few legal limits to confidentiality, like imminent risk of harm, are explained plainly in your intake paperwork before we begin. Read the HIPAA notice.

Why don't you take insurance?

Insurance requires a mental health diagnosis on your permanent record and often limits how many sessions you can have. Private pay keeps those decisions between you and your therapist. If your plan has out-of-network benefits, a superbill can be provided for you to submit. If cost is a barrier, ask about the sliding scale at your consult.

Do you see teenagers?

Yes, ages 12 and up, by video, with a parent or guardian's consent. Teens get the same confidential space as adults, and Jackie will explain to both of you what stays private and what does not.

Do you offer Christian-based counseling?

Yes, on request. If your faith is part of how you make sense of life, it can be part of your therapy. If it isn't, it never comes up. You decide.

What happens in the first session?

Mostly talking. You share what brought you in, at whatever depth feels right, and Jackie asks questions to understand the full picture. By the end you will have a sense of what working together could look like.

What if we're not the right fit?

That is part of what the free consult is for. If it turns out another kind of support would serve you better, Jackie will say so and point you toward it.

Can a doctor, school or family member refer someone to you?

Yes. Physicians, school counselors, therapists at capacity, clergy and family members refer clients regularly. The referral form asks only for a first name and a way to reach the person, who must know about the referral and agree to be contacted. Jackie reaches out within one business day. Make a referral.

How do I reschedule or cancel?

Let Jackie know at least 24 hours ahead and there is no charge. Later than that, the session fee applies, with exceptions for emergencies.
