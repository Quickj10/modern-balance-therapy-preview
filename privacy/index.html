How this website handles your information

Website privacy policy.

Last updated November 18, 2026

Draft for reviewHave your attorney or professional liability carrier confirm this before launch. Highlighted items need Jackie's input.

In short

  • This site collects only what you type into a form and the basic technical logs every web host keeps. No advertising cookies, no trackers.
  • Your details are used to reply to you and to schedule, never sold or shared for marketing.
  • This policy covers the website. Your care as a client is covered by the HIPAA Notice of Privacy Practices.

Who this policy covers

This policy explains what happens to information you share through the Modern Balance Therapy website (modernbalancetherapy.com), operated by Jackie McGreggor, MA, LPC, a Licensed Professional Counselor in Texas. It applies to visitors, people who request a consult, and people who send a referral. It does not replace the HIPAA Notice of Privacy Practices, which governs the health information of clients once care begins.

What the website collects

  • What you type. If you use the consult form or the referral form, the site collects the fields you fill in: your name, email address, phone number, the kind of help you are looking for, your availability, and anything you write in the message box. The referral form also collects the first name of the person being referred and one way to reach them.
  • Basic technical logs. Like every website, the host records standard server logs: IP address, browser type, pages visited, and timestamps. These are used for security and to keep the site running, not to identify you.
  • Nothing else. The site does not use advertising cookies, social media pixels, or behavioral trackers. If analytics are added in the future, this policy will be updated first and any cookie that is not strictly necessary will ask for your consent.

How your information is used

Form submissions are used for one purpose: to respond to you and, if you want, to schedule a free consultation or a first session. Referral details are used to reach the person referred and to let the referrer know contact was made. The practice does not sell personal information and does not share it with third parties for their own marketing.

Where it goes and how it is stored

Form submissions are delivered to the practice's email and scheduling system. [Name the platform and email provider in use, for example SimplePractice and Google Workspace] Session scheduling, intake paperwork and clinical records live in the practice's HIPAA-compliant practice management platform, not on this website. The website itself does not keep a database of what you submit.

Please do not include diagnoses, medical records or other sensitive health details in a website form. Those belong in a phone call or in the secure client portal once you are a client.

Third parties that help run the site

  • Web hosting, which stores the pages and keeps the server logs described above.
  • Email provider, which delivers form submissions to the practice.
  • Video platform for telehealth sessions, which is covered by a Business Associate Agreement under HIPAA and by the HIPAA notice, not this policy.
  • Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which may log the request in order to serve the font files.

How long it is kept

Consult and referral requests are kept for as long as needed to respond and schedule, and then for a reasonable period in case you reach out again. If you become a client, your information becomes part of your clinical record and is kept for the period Texas law requires for counseling records. If you never become a client, you can ask for your form submission to be deleted at any time.

Your choices and rights

  • You can ask what information the practice holds about you from the website, and ask for it to be corrected or deleted, by emailing jackie@modernbalancetherapy.com.
  • You can contact the practice by phone instead of using any form.
  • Texas residents and visitors from other jurisdictions may have additional rights under their local privacy laws; requests are honored to the extent the law allows and clinical record-keeping rules permit.

Children and teens

The practice sees clients ages 12 and up by telehealth with a parent or guardian's consent. This website is not directed at children under 13, and the practice does not knowingly collect information from a child under 13 through the website. If a parent or guardian is completing a form on behalf of a teen, they should enter their own contact details.

Security

The website is served over an encrypted connection (HTTPS). Form submissions are protected in transit. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, which is one more reason to keep clinical detail out of website forms and in the secure platforms built for it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version is posted here with an updated date at the top. Material changes to how information is collected or used will be flagged on this page.
