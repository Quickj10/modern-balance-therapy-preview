For providers, schools, clergy, and families

Make a referral.

Thank you for thinking of Modern Balance Therapy for someone in your care. I welcome referrals from physicians and pediatricians, school counselors, therapists at capacity, pastors and ministry leaders, employee assistance programs, and family members who have the person's consent.

What I take referrals for

  • Trauma and PTSD Specialty
  • Anxiety and overthinking
  • Depression and mood
  • Life transitions and grief
  • Relationship and family issues
  • Individuals 12 and up, and couples, including pre-marital
  • Parent coaching

Not a fit: active psychiatric crisis, court-ordered evaluations, psychological testing, medication management, or anyone located outside Texas. I will point you to a better resource in those cases.

How it works

  1. You send the formOr call or email, whichever is faster for you. First name and a way to reach the client is enough.
  2. I reach out within one business dayA short, low-pressure call to hear what is going on and offer a free 15-minute consult.
  3. If I am not the right fit, I say soYou and the client both get a community referral, so nobody starts over from nothing.

Good to know for referring providers

  • Secure, HIPAA-compliant video sessions, anywhere in Texas
  • Evening and weekend times available
  • Private pay: $150 to $175 individual, $200 couples
  • Superbills on request for out-of-network benefits
  • Good Faith Estimate provided in writing before the first session
  • Coordination of care with a signed release
